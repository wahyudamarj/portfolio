A Strong Opinion
Needs Support

Having a view is easy. Backing it up is the real work.

Three parts

Turn your opinion into an argument.

Claim What should happen? A human should make the final scholarship decision.
Evidence What backs that up? The only explanation you got was one line: the system ranked other applicants higher.
Reasoning Why does that matter? Because a decision this big should have a way to be checked.

Now people can check your argument, not just your opinion.
