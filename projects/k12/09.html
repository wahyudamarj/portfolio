1 · IDENTIFY

What's one thing that can make an everyday moment harder than it needs to be?

Think: too slow, hard to find, confusing, comes too late, wasteful, or hard for some people…
2 · DISTINGUISH

What's the difference between something you saw and something you're guessing?

One you can actually see in front of you; the other you're guessing about.
